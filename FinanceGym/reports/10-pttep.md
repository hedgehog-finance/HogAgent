# PTTEP: Do the 2025 Portfolio Moves Make 900,000 boe/d by 2030 Feasible?

**Research date basis: evidence published on or before 21 July 2025. Facts, inference and scenarios are labelled separately.**

## 1. Direct answer

The three named strategic actions are **directionally consistent with PTTEP's stated gas/LNG-weighted strategy but numerically immaterial to a 900,000 boe/d 2030 outcome**. On the admitted evidence, the Sinphuhorm buy-in adds roughly **1,700–2,000 boe/d**, a quantified set of other disclosed adders (Algeria Touat, Arthit contract uplift, Hassi Bir Rekaiz phase 2) totals only about **45,000–50,000 boe/d**, versus a gap of ~**393,000 boe/d** between the 2025 budget (507,000 boe/d) and 900,000. That is ~**12%** of the gap. Feasibility therefore does not hinge on these deals; it hinges on (a) holding the Erawan–Bongkot plateau against steep mature-field decline, (b) 2026–2027+ ramp-ups (Ghasha/Dalma, Abu Dhabi Waset, Malaysia greenfields, Algeria phase 2, Mozambique restart), and (c) further large acquisitions not disclosed pre-cutoff. **On pre-cutoff evidence, 900,000 boe/d in 2030 looks infeasible without a step-change in capital deployment that PTTEP's own disclosed budgets do not yet support.** Note also that no admitted pre-cutoff document states a "900,000 boe/d by 2030" corporate target (see §7).

## 2. Facts: the documented volume trajectory and capital plan

- 2024 average sales volume **488,794 boe/d**, +6% y/y; 2024 revenue THB 327,415 mn (USD 9,273 mn); net profit THB 78,824 mn (USD 2,227 mn) — [PTTEP, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.pttep.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/1039/pttep-announces-2024-performance-and-2025-operational-plan-investing-over-thb-261-billion-to-continuously-ensure-national-energy-security).
- Q1/2025 average sales volume **484,218 boe/d**, +2% y/y, "primarily driven by higher petroleum production from the G1/61 Project"; realised price USD 45.74/boe — [PTTEP, 25 Apr 2025](https://www.pttep.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/1053/pttep-reports-operating-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-2025).
- Company budget (Stock Exchange of Thailand filing of 20 Dec 2024): **507,000 boe/d in 2025, rising to 541,000 boe/d in 2026**; 2025 capex **USD 5.3 bn** of a **USD 21.2 bn five-year plan**; total 2025 capex+opex **USD 7.82 bn**, five-year **USD 33.6 bn** — [JPT/SPE, 8 Jan 2025](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects). (A separate pre-cutoff company summary cited 556,000 boe/d for 2026 — [PTT/NBT, 22 Aug 2024](https://www.facebook.com/nbtworld/posts/ptt-public-company-limited-ptt-has-emphasized-its-commitment-to-reducing-greenho/891594056338059) — an unreconciled discrepancy.)
- Investor deck series (sales volume, '000 boe/d): **2023: 462; 2024: 489; 1H2025: 495**; gas mix **72%**; asset value USD 28.6 bn (June 2025) — [PTTEP at a Glance, cached 2025 edition](https://hub.optiwise.io/storage/129/pttep-at-a-glance/2025/pttep-at-a-glance-3q2025.pdf).

**Inference (arithmetic, `math_calc`):** 507 → 900 requires **12.2%/yr for five years**; from the 2026 plan, **13.6%/yr for four years**. Actual growth 2023 → 2026 plan (462 → 541) was **5.4%/yr** — i.e. the target needs **more than double** the growth rate PTTEP has been delivering and budgeting.

## 3. The three actions, quantified

**Sinphuhorm (Thailand, onshore).** Facts: PTTEP's participating interest rose **80.487% → 90%** effective 16 April 2025, buying Jadestone's 9.52% via APICO LLC for **USD 39.4 mn** cash (USD 35 mn base, effective 1 Jan 2025) plus **USD 3.5 mn** contingent on licence extensions (licences expire 2029 and 2031) — [Jadestone, 16 Apr 2025](https://www.jadestone-energy.com/portfolio-focus-and-optimization-sale-of-sinphuhorm-interest); field produced **105 MMSCFD gas + 222 bbl/d condensate** in 2024 — [PTTEP, 25 Apr 2025](https://www.pttep.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/1053/pttep-reports-operating-results-for-the-first-quarter-of-2025). Inference: 9.52% × (105,000/6 + 222) ≈ **1,687 boe/d**; at the December-2024 gross rate of 122 MMscfd ([OGJ, 24 Mar 2025](https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/article/55276865/horizon-matahio-energy-to-acquire-assets-onshore-thailand-from-exxonmobil)) ≈ **~2,000 boe/d**, i.e. **0.2%** of the 2030 gap and **0.4%** of the 2025 budget. Strategically it consolidates operatorship and adds low-cost, low-decline dry gas (82% modelled recovery factor per OGJ), but it is a reserve/cost-per-unit and energy-security action, not a growth action. The 2029/2031 licence expiries also mean much of the added barrel could roll off at or just before the target year.

**North American divestment.** Gap: **no admitted pre-cutoff source documents a completed or announced "North American" divestment by PTTEP.** The closest admitted evidence is PTTEP's own disclosure that **Australia and Mexico positions are "under relinquishment approval"** in the same investor deck, the long-standing strategy of "rationaliz[ing] our investment portfolio in non-core areas" (e.g. Montara divestment, renegotiated Mariana oil-sands obligations — [PTTEP 2018 Annual Report](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/Z6QXO5CYQFLRE9D9/550357_2018PTTEP%20Annual%20Report.pdf)), and the 2025 pivot of capital toward UAE/Algeria/Malaysia/Mozambique — [JPT/SPE, 8 Jan 2025](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects). Inference: a North American exit is **volume-dilutive in the near term** (it removes oil-weighted barrels) while being margin-, capital-efficiency- and carbon-intensity-accretive. It therefore makes a *boe-count* target harder and a *value/gas-mix* objective easier — a real tension in the question's framing.

**Sarawak / Malaysia drilling.** Facts: PTTEP's 2025 workplan "accelerate[s] development projects in Malaysia Greenfields ... while fast-tracking exploration projects in both Thailand and Malaysia" ([PTTEP, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.pttep.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/1039/pttep-announces-2024-performance-and-2025-operational-plan-investing-over-thb-261-billion-to-continuously-ensure-national-energy-security)); the plan names production start-up at Malaysian greenfield projects **SK405B, SK417, SK438** ([JPT/SPE, 8 Jan 2025](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects)); **SK410B (Lang Lebah)** was under **re-engineering within 2025** "for cost reduction and optimal balance between production capacity and investment" ([PTTEP at a Glance](https://hub.optiwise.io/storage/129/pttep-at-a-glance/2025/pttep-at-a-glance-3q2025.pdf)). The regional activity backdrop is PETRONAS targeting **2 MMboe/d by 2027** with ~15 exploration wells/year ([industry summary, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aznial-rahmat-a3145636_petronas-activity-outlook-for-2025-2027-petronass-activity-7290897104997072896-7m8W) — secondary source). Gap: **no admitted source quantifies Sarawak well counts, volumes or first-gas dates for PTTEP.** Inference: deepwater gas projects sanctioned/re-designed in 2025 typically deliver first gas in **2028–2031**, so Sarawak drilling is largely **post-2030 optionality** and a *reserve replacement* (R/P > 5 years is a stated long-term target — [PTTEP sustainability framework](https://sustainability.pttep.com/en/our-sustainability/sustainability-framework-and-long-term-target)) rather than a 2030 volume lever.

## 4. Erawan: the swing factor

Facts: G1/61 (Erawan, Platong, Satun, Funan) rose **210 → 400 MMSCFD by end-Q2 2023** ([OGJ, 31 Jul 2023](https://www.ogj.com/ogj-survey-downloads/worldwide-production/article/14297140/pttep-increases-natural-gas-production-from-g1-g6-gulf-of-thailand-project)) and **400 → 800 MMSCFD since 20 March 2024**, sustained through 2024–2025 ([PTTEP, 30 Jan 2025](https://www.pttep.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/1039/pttep-announces-2024-performance-and-2025-operational-plan-investing-over-thb-261-billion-to-continuously-ensure-national-energy-security); [Nation Thailand, 20 Mar 2024](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/corporate/40036567)). PTTEP holds a **60% operated interest** (Mubadala 40%); the Erawan/Bongkot pair supplies ~**60% of domestic gas consumption**, with an explicit commitment to hold ~**1,500 MMcf/d** combined ([JPT/SPE, 8 Jan 2025](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects)); capital to reverse the decline only began in **2022** after Chevron-transfer delays, and in 2023 the plan was "maintain gas sales at 400 MMSCFD ... continue drilling new production wells" plus four wellhead platforms ([PTTEP opportunity-day deck, 2 Nov 2023](http://www.chemwinfo.com/private_folder/2023/23_Nov/Pttep_RS_oppday-20231102-0119213777.pdf)).

Inference: 800 MMSCFD ≈ **133,000 boe/d** of gas-equivalent, i.e. ~**26%** of the 2025 budget in one project. The "recovery challenge" is therefore a **plateau problem, not a ramp problem**: at an assumed 10% natural decline from that plateau (analyst assumption, not from sources), PTTEP would surrender ~13,000 boe/d per year unless infill drilling and new platforms keep pace — the same volume the entire Sinphuhorm deal added, every five weeks. More broadly, offsetting a ~5% base decline on the 72% gas-weighted book costs roughly **18,000 boe/d per year** (~90,000 boe/d over five years) before any growth is achieved.

## 5. Scenario bridges to 2030 (illustrative, built on admitted 2025–26 anchors)

| Scenario | 2030 outcome | Assumptions |
|---|---|---|
| **Stall** | ~**609,000 boe/d** | +3%/yr after 2026; Erawan decays from 800 MMSCFD; Mozambique late; greenfields slip |
| **Base execution** | ~**679,000 boe/d** | 6%/yr off 507: Erawan plateau held, Touat ~16,000 boe/d ([OGJ/PTTEP](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects)), Ghasha/Dalma first gas early 2026, HBR phase-2 ramp to 30 KBPD (2029) — [PTTEP MD&A, 31 Jan 2024](https://market.sec.or.th/public/idisc/Download?FILEID=dat%2Fnews%2F202401%2F0355NWS300120241805050120E.pdf) |
| **Bull** | ~**817,000 boe/d** | 10%/yr: Waset first gas 2027, SK405B/SK417/SK438 startups, MTJDA expansion, strong pricing |
| **Target-consistent** | **900,000 boe/d** | Requires **12.2%/yr** and ~**393,000 boe/d** of net additions — only ~12% of which is quantified in disclosed projects |

Mechanism summary: divestment proceeds and relinquished non-core capital are **redeployed** into gas/LNG where PTTEP earns 70–75% EBITDA margins and long-term GSAs ([PTTEP at a Glance](https://hub.optiwise.io/storage/129/pttep-at-a-glance/2025/pttep-at-a-glance-3q2025.pdf)); acquisitions of partners' stakes (Bongkot 100% after TotalEnergies' 33.33% transfer, Sinphuhorm 90%) **raise net share of existing barrels without exploration risk**; drilling in Sarawak/Thailand **replenishes reserves** with a 3–5 year lag. Counterargument: each lever either shrinks the barrel count (exits), adds trivially (Sinphuhorm), or matures after 2030 (deepwater), while USD 5.3 bn/yr capex is already committed to a 507→541 trajectory — leaving no disclosed funding envelope for a doubling.

## 6. Uncertainties

Volume conversion (boe) uses 6 Mcf = 1 boe; PTTEP's own deck states gas is ~72% of sales; budget vs. 1H25 actual (507 vs 495) shows the company running ~2% below plan; realised price falling to USD 45.74/boe (Q1/25) pressures the self-funding of capex out of 2024's USD 2.2 bn net profit; Mozambique remained under force majeure pending restart guarantees ([JPT/SPE](https://jpt.spe.org/pttep-2025-capex-targets-upstream-gas-lng-projects)).

## 7. Verification limits and gaps

1. **The 900,000 boe/d 2030 target itself is unverified** in admitted pre-cutoff sources; disclosed official guidance stops at 2026 (541,000 boe/d), and stated long-term targets are qualitative (growth, R/P > 5 years, net zero 2050).
2. **No admitted source confirms a North American divestment** — analysis is framed as a portfolio logic, not an established event.
3. **No admitted source quantifies PTTEP's Sarawak drilling programme**; one targeted search returned no results (coverage gap, not evidence of absence).
4. **Skill-based market data unavailable:** the Finnhub news call for PTTEP.BK failed (CLI exit 1); OpenBB was not used as index/FX/yield data are not material here. Consequently no market-price or analyst-expectation evidence is included, and no valuation model was run.
5. The PTTEP "at a Glance" PDF cached under a June-2025 stamp carries "last update: July 2025" markings; only items clearly dated on/before 21 July 2025 were used, and post-cutoff lines (e.g. 25/31 July 2025) were excluded.
