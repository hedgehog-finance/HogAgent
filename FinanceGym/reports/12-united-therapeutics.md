# Tyvaso Concentration, the Failed Liquidia Injunctions, and Whether an IPF Label Can Protect UTHR Through 2026

**Information cutoff: 2025-08-05. Facts below are limited to documents published on or before that date. Inference and forward scenarios are explicitly labelled.**

## Direct answer

An IPF label expansion is a *real but slow-acting* hedge against a *fast-acting* competitive threat. As of the cutoff, United Therapeutics' (UTHR) exposure was exactly as the premise states — total Tyvaso revenue of **$469.6m on total revenue of $798.6m in Q2 2025 = 58.8%** of sales ([Q2 2025 release](https://ir.unither.com/press-releases/2025/07-30-2025-113026622)) — and Liquidia's Yutrepia was already shipping to patients with **no injunction outstanding**, because UTHR's emergency-relief motions had been denied ([Liquidia, 2 Jun 2025](https://liquidia.com/news-releases/news-release-details/liquidia-schedules-first-commercial-shipment-yutrepiatm)). But the IPF pathway (the **TETON** programme) could not contribute revenue inside the 2026 window: TETON 2 data were guided to **September 2025**, TETON 1 data to **first half 2026**, with filing and review still ahead ([Q2 2025 release](https://ir.unither.com/press-releases/2025/07-30-2025-113026622); [TETON 1 enrolment, 4 Feb 2025](https://ir.unither.com/press-releases/2025/02-04-2025-120131472)). So through 2026 the IPF option protects valuation mainly **through the cost of equity narrative — pipeline optionality that offsets a temporarily eroding cash-flow base — not through revenue.** Its revenue power only becomes credible for 2027+.

Two mechanisms do most of the medium-term protection: (i) IPF addresses a US population of **">100,000 patients"** with **only two approved drugs, which "only slow the course of disease progression"** ([TETON 1 release](https://ir.unither.com/press-releases/2025/02-04-2025-120131472)) — an order of magnitude larger than the ~8,800-patient Tyvaso base estimated by a third party ([Bleecker Street, 29 Jul 2025](https://www.bleeckerstreetresearch.com/research/uthr-lqda)); and (ii) the same molecule, device and field force are reused, so incremental patients are almost pure margin.

## Verified facts

**Concentration is high and rising.** Tyvaso was 45.1% of revenue in 2022 ($873m) ([Bloomberg Law, 20 Dec 2023](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/liquidias-yutrepia-path-clear-as-utc-loses-axed-patent-appeal)), 56.3% in 2024 ($1,620.4m of ~$2.88bn, +31% y/y) ([4Q24 release, 26 Feb 2025](https://ir.unither.com/~/media/Files/U/United-Therapeutics-IR/documents/press-releases/2024/2025-02-26-4q24-earnings-release.pdf)) and 58.8% in Q2 2025. The franchise still grew 18% y/y (DPI +22%, nebulized +10%) even after Yutrepia launched; Remodulin fell 9%.

**Litigation state.** Yutrepia was approved **23 May 2025** for PAH *and* PH-ILD on the same day Tyvaso DPI's new-clinical-investigation exclusivity expired ([Liquidia 10-K, 19 Mar 2025](https://www.liquidia.com/static-files/3e01efd2-5158-4131-830d-63108709df42); [Hunterbrook, 23 May 2025](https://hntrbrk.com/breaking-news/liquidia-united)); first commercial shipment followed **2 June 2025**. On **30 May 2025** the M.D.N.C. denied UTHR's TRO/preliminary injunction on the '782 patent, finding UTHR "not likely to succeed on the merits," that lost sales were "speculative," and that the public interest favoured denial ([Robins Kaplan Q2 2025 bulletin](https://www.robinskaplan.com/newsroom/insights/generically-speaking-hatch-waxman-bulletin-2025-q2-united-therapeutics-corp-v-liquidia-techs-inc)). That followed a denied PI in Delaware on the '327 patent (31 May 2024), the Federal Circuit's invalidation of '793, and an October 2024 Supreme Court refusal — hence "a series." **Important qualification: the merits cases are not over.** UTHR still asserts patents expiring as late as January 2042, and Liquidia's own filings warn an adverse ruling could still restrict Yutrepia to PAH or force royalties/damages ([Liquidia 10-K](https://www.liquidia.com/static-files/3e01efd2-5158-4131-830d-63108709df42)).

**Balance-sheet shock absorbers (facts).** At 30 June 2025: cash, equivalents and marketable investments **$4,966.4m**, total liabilities **$734.4m**, equity **$7,173.6m**, diluted EPS $6.41, operating margin 45.6% ($364.5m/$798.6m), plus a **$1bn buyback** authorised to 31 Mar 2026 — explicitly framed by the board as confidence in "upcoming catalysts" and "our share price potential" ([Q2 2025 release](https://ir.unither.com/press-releases/2025/07-30-2025-113026622)).

## Mechanisms, counterarguments, scenarios

**Why the IPF label mitigates.** (1) Diversification: a new indication changes the 58.8% single-product dependency into one product across three indications, so Yutrepia — a *DPI* — competes only with Tyvaso DPI, while a nebulized IPF label opens a channel Liquidia cannot copy without its own IPF trials. (2) Duration: the IPF label is supported by UTHR's own patents/programme rather than the Orange Book patents already lost or doubted, and PPF plus an EMA filing (partner Ferrer) extend the runway. (3) Multiple: for a company with $4.97bn liquidity, an approved IPF drug shifts the terminal-growth assumption for the largest revenue line, which matters far more in a valuation than one quarter's Tyvaso growth.

**Counterarguments.** (a) *Timing mismatch*: with TETON 2 in Sept 2025 and TETON 1 in 1H 2026, plus bridging studies for DPI, no material IPF revenue is plausible in 2026 — mitigation is sentiment, not cash flow. (b) *Binary risk*: FVC-based IPF endpoints can miss; INCREASE post-hoc gains (+2.5% at week 8, +3.5% at week 16) were subgroup signals, not registration evidence. (c) *Substitution, not addition*: physicians in the July survey flagged Yutrepia's tolerability and dosing flexibility; the same argument cuts against treprostinil in IPF, where cough/bronchospasm warnings are prominent. (d) *Litigation overhang cuts both ways*: if UTHR later wins on the merits, damages/injunctions help cash flow but signal enforcement risk; if it loses, DPI erosion persists beyond 2026. (e) *IPF incumbents* (two approved antifibrotics) anchor payer willingness-to-pay.

**Scenarios for 2026.** Bull: TETON 2 and TETON 1 positive, sNDA accepted, DPI bridging path clear → concentration falls below ~50% on a forward basis and the 5%–30% share-loss debate becomes immaterial. Base: TETON 2 positive, TETON 1 mixed/late → modest 2026 Tyvaso share loss (~$178m) offset by Orenitram/Unituxin growth and the buyback; IPF revenue lands 2027. Bear: TETON misses plus Yutrepia accelerates toward the 28% case (~$454m, ~$4.29 EPS) with no near-term IPF offset.
