# AppLovin vs. the Nasdaq, March–July 2025: Premise Check, Measured Relative Performance, and the Tesla Comparison

**Cutoff: 2025-07-09 (inclusive). Facts below are limited to evidence dated on or before the cutoff.**

## 1. Direct answer

**The premise is not supported.** AppLovin (APP) did not deliver "consistent triple-digit alpha" against the Nasdaq between March and July 2025. Measured against the Nasdaq Composite index history retrieved for the window, the index returned **+11.27%** from 3 Mar (close 18,350.19) to 8 Jul 2025 (20,418.46), or **+12.32%** to the 9 Jul close (20,611.34) ([OpenBB/yfinance history](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/%5EIXIC/history/)). Every dated APP price anchor available before the cutoff implies relative outperformance in the **single-digit to mid-30s percentage-point range**, not 100+ points — and the outperformance was **episodic, not consistent**, with two violent single-day *under*performance events inside the window.

What *is* real and well-evidenced: a genuine **fundamental divergence between AppLovin and Tesla (TSLA)** in Q1 2025 — accelerating, hyper-high-margin advertising growth versus shrinking, margin-collapse automotive revenue. That divergence, however, is a story about *fundamentals and narrative*, not about low beta: APP was itself an extremely high-volatility, high-beta-style name whose index-relative moves were driven by company-specific catalysts.

## 3. Mechanisms behind the (smaller, lumpy) outperformance

2. **Portfolio simplification plus optionality.** Sale of the mobile-gaming (Apps) business to Tripledot for **$400m cash + ~20% equity**, and a shift to **advertising-only guidance** (2Q25: revenue $1.195–1.215bn, adj. So AppLovin = accelerating revenue/margin/FCF beating consensus; Tesla = contracting revenue and margins missing consensus.

**Risk profile: no, not a divergence — that is the wrong axis.** Both were narrative-, event-, and headline-driven names. AppLovin printed a −20% all-time-record drop and an −8.2% index-divergent day *inside the window*, and had "60 moves greater than 5% over the last year" ([Yahoo/Fool, 9 Jun 2025](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/why-applovin-app-stock-falling-164517429.html)). Tesla, meanwhile, **rose** on a dismal Q1 print (+~5% after hours on the Powell headline), showing that both traded on narrative rather than reported fundamentals ([LeverageShares, 29 Apr 2025](https://leverageshares.com/us/insights/why-teslas-stock-rose-despite-dismal-q1-earnings)).
