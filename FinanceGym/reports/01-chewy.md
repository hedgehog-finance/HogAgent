# Chewy (NYSE: CHWY) — Did CEO/CTO Insider Selling of ~$67M Signal Misalignment with "Strong Active Customer Growth"?

**Point-in-time research note. Evidence restricted to publications dated on or before 2025-04-28. Not investment advice.**

## Direct answer

The dollar magnitude in the premise is **verifiable**: Form 4 data retrieved for the window ending 2025-04-28 show open-market-coded sales ("S") by CEO Sumit Singh of **$50.97M (1,429,467 shares)** and by CTO Satish Mehta of **$16.55M (444,053 shares)** — **$67.52M combined**. However, these sales **only weakly support** a claim of executive misalignment with Chewy's reported customer-growth metrics, for three evidenced reasons: (1) both executives sold under **pre-existing Rule 10b5-1 plans adopted in mid-2024**, before the Q4/FY2024 results; (2) gross selling was largely **offset by same-window equity-award vesting** (coded "A": Singh 1,223,324 shares; Mehta 224,236 shares), so net share reduction was far smaller; and (3) no pre-cutoff filing or disclosure links the sales to deteriorating customer metrics. The genuinely uncomfortable fact is narrower and more mechanical: the selling was **concentrated into a Dec-2024–Jan-2025 price spike** (sales printed between $32.50 and $40.00), while the descriptor "strong active customer growth" was applied to a **turn from −1.6% to +2.1%** in a 364-day look-back metric that was still ~0.9% below its FY2021 level. That combination justifies *moderate* scepticism about how management framed the metric — not an inference of informed distribution.

## Mechanisms: how incentives could plausibly drive selling

1. **Equity-heavy, time-vested pay.** Singh's April 2024 grant comprised 829,793 RSUs plus 628,661 PRSUs (0–200% payout, vesting 2027-02-01); 2023 annual incentive paid at 131% of a $1.8M target (proxy, 2024-05-13). With pay delivered in stock and quarterly vest tranches (e.g., Feb/May/Aug/Nov/Dec 2025 dates), selling is mechanically required to fund liquidity and taxes. Inference: **timing is more explainable by vesting calendars than by forecast revisions** — 86% of Singh's gross shares sold in the window were matched by awards in the same window.
2. **Selling into strength / valuation ceiling.** Prices printed $40.00 on 2025-01-30 versus $32.50 on 2024-12-19 and $33.35 on 2025-04-02; the stock was described as up ~88% year-over-year on 2025-03-10. At $33.35 and 413.6M A+B shares, market cap ≈ **$13.8B**, and EV/adj. EBITDA ≈ **23.1× FY2024** or **19.2× guided FY2025 EBITDA** (my calculation from 10-K balance sheet/PR figures plus guidance). Inference: plan sales at elevated prices are consistent with rational diversification, which is *also* what a bear would do.
3. **Metric-quality channel (the real misalignment risk).** The 364-day look-back means an "active customer" persists for a year after their last order; churn registers only with a lag, and low-spend new cohorts dilute NSPAC — UBS noted new customers "spend well below existing customers." Guided +6.4% 52-week-comparable sales growth, assuming NSPAC repeats +4.1%, implies only **≈2.3% active-customer growth** (my calculation) — i.e., management's own guide does not assume a step-change. If incentives are scored on a headline customer-count inflection, a low-quality +2.1% print can be characterized as "strong."
4. **Governance/liquidity demand channel.** Post-IPO founder/sponsor entities (CIE Management IX, Argos-related holders) were converting and selling tens of millions of shares, indicating a broader shareholder de-risking program in which executive sales are a small tail ($67.5M ≈ **0.49%** of market cap; my calculation).

## Counterarguments

- **Pre-commitment removes signal value.** Plans adopted July 12 and September 25, 2024 predate the FY2024 beat (March 26, 2025); Mehta's disclosed 338,130-share authorization and the Feb 5–6, 2025 sales described as covering RSU tax obligations (Investing.com, 2025-02-07) support a liquidity/tax reading.
- **Pace collapsed after the "strong growth" statement.** Only one post-results sale appears before the cutoff: Singh, 8,552 shares @ $33.35 (≈$0.29M, 2025-04-02) — tax-scale, not liquidation-scale. If Singh held private doubts about the March 26 framing, the March-26–April-28 record shows no escalation.
- **Metrics were corroborated by non-customer data** in the same release: Autoship sales +21.2% in Q4, app installs +20%, first app purchasers +34%, FCF $452.5M, share gains flagged by UBS (Retail Dive, 2025-03-27).
- **Data caveats cut both ways.** Mehta's 444,053 gross sold shares exceed the 338,130 disclosed authorization, and the vendor feed codes several items ambiguously (award-related dispositions as "S"), so gross dollars overstate *discretionary* selling.

## Scenarios (forward-looking, explicitly not knowledge as of 2025-04-28)

- **Base:** sales continue as scheduled under plans through July 2025 expiry; the customer-growth turn persists at 1–3%, so the selling is judged ex post as routine. Signal value: low.
- **Bear:** active-customer growth reverts to declines (the FY2021→FY2024 CAGR was already **−0.30%/yr**), NSPAC decelerates, and the pre-print $40-area selling is re-read as distribution into a narrative inflection. Signal value: high — the decisive test would be FY2025 Q1–Q2 active-customer and NSPAC disclosures.
- **Bull:** Autoship/Chewy+ flywheel plus healthcare and share gains convert the +2.1% base into accelerating counts, making the December–January selling a non-event.
